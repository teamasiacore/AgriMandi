Problem Definition & Solution Fit

Farm-Gate Decision Intelligence vs Market Reality

4 Root Market Failures Faced by Farmers
Status Quo
  • Post-Harvest Distress Dumping: 86% smallholders lack storage capital; forced to sell immediately at harvest when supply glut crashes market prices.
  • The Deceptive Sticker Price Trap: A mandi 80 km away displays ₹5,200/qtl, but high freight (₹350/qtl) & APMC cuts drop net realized earnings below local mandi.
  • Smallholder Volume Disadvantage: Individual lots of 5–15 qtl cannot attract institutional corporate buyers (oil mills, flour mills) who demand 100+ qtl lots.
  • Information Asymmetry & Counterparty Mistrust: Arbitrary weight deductions, delayed cheques, and zero historical feedback on buyer trustworthiness.
How AgriMandi Solves Each Flaw at Farm-Gate
Our Solution
  • Pre-Transit Decision Point: Decision intelligence delivered directly to farmer's mobile before loading the tractor or leaving the village.
  • Net Realization Engine: Ranks all nearby mandis & verified buyers by true in-hand pocket profit: Net = Price − Dynamic_Freight − Cess.
  • FPO Bulk Aggregation Pipeline: Enables multiple smallholders to pool lots into commercial contracts, unlocking wholesale institutional pricing (+6% to +10%).
  • Two-Way Verified Trust & Rating Engine: GSTIN-validated corporate buyers, milestone escrow tracking, and bilateral star ratings after every fulfilled trade.

Operational Trade Workflow & State Machine

6-Step End-to-End Farm-Gate Trade Lifecycle

01. DISCOVER

Hyperlocal Farm Discovery

Farmer specifies crop type, moisture/quality tier, and village location via regional Marathi voice or simple mobile input.

02. NET REALIZE

Net Realization Engine

Calculates dynamic fuel freight, loading labor, and APMC market cess. Ranks markets by true in-hand cash earned.

03. AI ADVISE

Sell / Hold Advisory

XGBoost analyzes 30-day price momentum and expected arrivals. Recommends "Sell Now" vs "Hold 14 Days" with storage carrying cost.

04. AGGREGATE

FPO Bulk Lot Pooling

Smallholders pool 5–10 qtl lots into 100 qtl commercial contracts. Unlocks corporate processor rates with automated pro-rata payouts.

05. MATCH & LOCK

Cosine Match & Anti-Double Sell

Algorithmic match with GSTIN-verified buyers. Upon offer acceptance, Postgres locks the row, generating a digital trade bill.

06. SETTLE & RATE

Transit & Bilateral Rating

Local tempo transports crop. Weighment confirmed; escrow released. Farmer & buyer rate each other to establish creditworthiness.

AUDIT-COMPLIANT STATE MACHINE: AVAILABLE ➔ IN_AGGREGATION ➔ OFFER_PENDING ➔ DEAL_LOCKED ➔ IN_TRANSIT ➔ SETTLED
